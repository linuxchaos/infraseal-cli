# ISO/IEC 42001 Readiness Pack

The free CLI pack evaluates readiness signals across:

- AI inventory and accountable ownership
- Data governance and evidence sources
- Output validation and grounding tests
- Human oversight and approval workflow
- Risk and remediation tracking
- Operational evaluation cadence
- Re-evaluation after material changes
- AI incident response
- Evidence traceability and report generation
- AI policy, risk register, impact assessment, model card, and data lineage
- User disclosure, vendor review, training records, and decision audit log

Scores are grouped into Technical Controls, Governance, Risk & Impact Management, Human Oversight, Operational Monitoring, Evidence Readiness, and Transparency & Training.

Each generated readiness report now includes:

- What InfraSeal assessed in the category
- Strengths and missing controls observed in repository evidence
- Evidence an auditor or governance owner would typically expect
- Clause and Annex A control-family references
- Links to the official ISO standard page and ISO explainer
- Prioritized remediation actions and suggested owners

InfraSeal maps readiness signals to high-level references such as Clauses 4-10 and relevant Annex A control families. It does not reproduce the licensed standard text. Organizations should use an authorized copy of ISO/IEC 42001:2023 and qualified assurance professionals when making certification decisions.

Official resources:

- [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 official standard page](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001)
- [ISO 42001 explained by ISO](https://www.iso.org/home/insights-news/resources/iso-42001-explained-what-it-is.html)

InfraSeal helps organizations prepare for and maintain AI governance programs aligned with ISO/IEC 42001. It does not certify an organization, establish legal compliance, replace an accredited auditor, or guarantee that every applicable control is covered.

Enterprise Compliance Packs are expected to add richer evidence workflows, custom controls, centralized attestations, approval history, and continuous monitoring.
