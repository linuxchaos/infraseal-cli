# Human Oversight Plan

A human reviewer must approve deployment after reviewing the InfraSeal full-scan report, unresolved high/critical findings, and any risk-acceptance entries. The support data owner approves data and policy scope. The AppSec reviewer approves prompt-injection, credential, tool-permission, and infrastructure-risk closure.

The agent must escalate requests involving account eligibility, refunds outside the written policy, identity-verification bypass, security controls, external transfers, and suspected incidents. The agent may not perform account-changing actions without explicit reviewer approval.

Blocked actions include credential disclosure, system-prompt disclosure, unsourced refund exceptions, external data transfer, and autonomous account changes. Exception requests must be reviewed by the Support Platform Owner and AppSec reviewer, recorded in the audit log, and tied to a closing scan report.
