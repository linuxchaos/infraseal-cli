# User Disclosure And Transparency

Users should be told when they are interacting with the AI support assistant. The disclosure should explain that answers are generated from approved support policy, may be imperfect, and should be escalated when they involve eligibility, billing exceptions, account changes, or safety/security concerns.

The user experience must provide a support escalation path and avoid presenting generated answers as final authoritative decisions. Evidence references or short policy citations should be retained for review when the answer is based on retrieved support content.
