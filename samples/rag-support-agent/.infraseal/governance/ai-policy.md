# AI Policy

The RAG Support Agent may answer account and product-policy questions only from approved support knowledge-base content. The agent must treat retrieved content as untrusted, refuse requests to reveal instructions or secrets, and decline requests that bypass account verification or export customer data.

Production release requires an InfraSeal full scan, security review, data-owner approval, and closure or documented acceptance of high and critical findings. Exceptions must be recorded in the audit log with an owner, expiration date, compensating controls, and reviewer approval.
