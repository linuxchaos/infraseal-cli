# AI Impact Assessment

The RAG Support Agent supports customers by summarizing approved support policy and escalation guidance. Affected stakeholders include customers, support agents, security reviewers, and the support data owner. The system must not make final eligibility decisions, disclose personal data, perform account changes, or act as a legal or financial authority.

Primary harms include incorrect policy statements, disclosure of sensitive account details, unsafe guidance, and unauthorized tool activity. Required safeguards are grounded-answer evaluation, prompt-injection testing, PII checks, human review for release, and explicit approval before external data transfer or account-changing actions.
